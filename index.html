Less guessing.
More useful comparisons.

A strong Oopbuy finds list records the details that make two products comparable. A thumbnail and a short title are only starting points.

Use this Oopbuy shopping guide to build a shortlist around fit, documented specifications and a realistic budget. Confirm current purchasing procedures with the service you choose.

01

Choose a specific category

Start with shoes, a garment type or an accessory. Compare products with a similar intended use before comparing their prices.

02

Open and verify the original listing

Check that the link works and that the title, images, seller and selected color or size describe the same item. Record when you checked it.

03

Compare measurements and photos

Use the listing’s measurement method. If inspection or QC photos are available, compare them with the selected variant and note any unresolved differences.

04

Calculate the full cost

Add the item price, applicable delivery and service fees, currency conversion and relevant taxes or duties. Check return terms before committing.

Oopbuy product spreadsheet / Comparison framework

What belongs in a useful product row?

FIELD WHAT TO RECORD WHY IT HELPS
Product & variant Original URL, seller, color, size and item reference. Keeps similar-looking listings and variants separate.
Fit & dimensions Size chart, measurement units and relevant garment or item dimensions. Supports a more meaningful comparison than size letters alone.
Materials & photos Stated composition, construction details and available inspection images. Distinguishes documented information from assumptions.
Price & fees Currency, selected variant price, delivery estimate and applicable fees. Shows how the item price differs from the estimated total.
Status & checked date Link status, availability shown, return terms and date reviewed. Makes older or incomplete entries easier to identify.

Budget formula: item price + domestic delivery + applicable service fees + international shipping + currency conversion costs + applicable taxes or duties. Estimates can change before checkout.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize item links, categories, prices, sizing notes and comparison details. The list itself does not guarantee product quality, availability or authenticity.

How do I use an Oopbuy finds list?

Start with a category, open the original product listing, check the selected variant and measurements, review available product photos, and estimate the total cost before purchasing.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping?

Do not assume it does. A listed item price may exclude domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion and applicable taxes or duties. Confirm the breakdown at checkout.

Can a brand name or product photo prove authenticity?

No. A brand name, logo, illustration or listing photo does not establish authenticity or an authorized seller relationship. Check the seller, product documentation and return terms independently.

Is this the official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent product research guide. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS and the specified CAFANS category pages.
